# DiffeoCFM correlation adapter

The correlation adapter follows the architecture and statistical prior from
https://github.com/antoinecollas/DiffeoCFM at revision
`902282acd13a67784e2096d09ee93477b268c079`. It adapts preprocessing, RNGs,
validation, checkpointing and sampling diagnostics for CorrBench. The restricted
SPD baseline files are not included.
